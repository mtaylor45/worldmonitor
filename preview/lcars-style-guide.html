The elbow

SEC 01
OUTER SWEEP · 72PX INNER CARVE · 30PX ARM ≥ RAIL WIDTH

Every LCARS screen is a frame first and a canvas second. The elbow is the joint where the horizontal header meets the vertical rail, and it is the single form that identifies the language. Get it right and the screen reads correctly before any colour lands.

It is built from one block and one carve. The outer corner takes a wide radius. The inner corner is cut by a pseudo-element filled with the field colour, carrying its own smaller radius. No clip-path, no SVG — the construction stays crisp at any scale and costs nothing to composite.

The ratio matters more than the absolute values. The outer sweep runs roughly 2.4× the inner carve. Push them closer together and the joint reads as a plain rounded corner; push them apart and it reads as a bubble.

Colour

SEC 02

On screen, LCARS colour was decorative — Michael Okuda assigned it for rhythm, not meaning. A working dashboard cannot afford that. Each hue below carries one job, and the discipline of never spending a colour on decoration is what keeps the screen legible at a glance.

The strongest rule here is negative: salmon and red are status only. The moment either appears as ornament, an alert stops meaning anything.

Type

SEC 03

One family, ultra-condensed, all capitals. Canon used Swiss 911 Ultra Compressed; Antonio is the free substitute and the only face in the system. Condensed capitals are what let a label sit inside a 34px block without the block growing to accommodate it.

Cap height must equal block height. Because the em box carries leading the condensed face doesn't use, set font-size = row-height × 1.36 and verify optically. Padding is not the fix.

Page archetypes

SEC 05

Five layouts, each with a distinct structural signature so the page is identifiable from the doorway before any label is readable. The frame colour and the block rhythm carry that — not the content.

OPS

DEFAULT · MIXED DENSITY

The landing state. Balanced blocks, no single dominant panel, alert feed pinned along the bottom edge where peripheral vision catches colour change. This is the page that must survive being glanced at.

LONG RANGE SCAN

MAP DOMINANT · MUTED

One panel takes the whole well; everything else compresses into the frame. Runs the Drexler palette so the map's own colour carries. A segmented scrubber sits directly above the well.

ENGINEERING

SYMMETRIC · METERED

Vertical meters in matched columns, gold frame. The symmetry is the signal: a broken column in an otherwise regular grid is visible from across the room without reading a number.

COMMS

LIST · UNIFORM ROWS

Feed traffic in even rows, lilac frame, newest at the top. The only page where uniformity is correct — irregular rows here would imply a hierarchy the content doesn't have.

LIBRARY

DENSE · DUAL RAIL

The only archetype with rails on both edges, framing three columns of dense text and tabular data. Reserved for pages someone will actually stand and read — reference material, system detail, history. Everywhere else, a second rail steals width the content needs.

Motion

SEC 06

The governing rule: LCARS cuts, it does not fade. The originals were physical backlit panels — a state change was a lamp switching, so it was instantaneous. No easing curves, no transforms, no cross-fades. Four kinds of motion are permitted, and every one of them reports something.

Everything below honours prefers-reduced-motion. On an always-on panel in living space, ambient motion is a cost paid every hour of the day.

BLOCK BLINK

A single block changing colour on a hard cut. Signals activity on that channel. One block at a time, never a cascade.

SWEEP

Linear fill, constant rate, no easing. Only for genuine progress — an indeterminate sweep is a lie about a known quantity.

SEQUENTIAL REVEAL

Blocks light in order at ~40ms intervals. Reserved for boot and for waking from ambient. The system's one theatrical moment.

ALERT PULSE

STANDING BY

Hard alternation between critical red and field, roughly 1Hz. The only motion permitted to run unattended, and only while the condition holds.

DIGIT CYCLE

47-1904-2839

Decorative codes reshuffling. Use on ornamental filler only. Never on a code beside a control someone is learning to find.

FORBIDDEN

Fades between states. Scale or translate transforms. Easing curves of any kind. Hover transitions on every block. Skeleton shimmer. Anything that suggests a surface is soft, elastic, or physical — these are lit panels, not objects.

Rules

SEC 07

DO

  • Hold the 5px gutter absolutely. Two coloured blocks never touch.
  • Round every outer corner of the frame; leave inner corners carved or square.
  • Align labels bottom-right inside their block, on the block floor.
  • Let text interrupt a bar rather than sit on top of it.
  • Build the frame first. Content fills what the frame leaves.
  • Give each page a distinct frame colour and block rhythm.
  • Keep status tags rectangular so they stand out among pills.
  • Derive decorative codes deterministically so they hold still.
  • Lift the field to #090909 rather than pure black.

DON'T

  • Spend salmon or red on anything that isn't a status.
  • Fade, ease, scale or slide. State changes are cuts.
  • Put a border or shadow on a block — the gutter is the separation.
  • Mix a second typeface. One condensed family, all caps.
  • Drop below 13px on a 163-PPI panel.
  • Let number dumps sit where they could be mistaken for data.
  • Round a status tag or square a frame corner.
  • Use more than two pill buttons on one screen.
  • Add a second rail outside the LIBRARY archetype.
